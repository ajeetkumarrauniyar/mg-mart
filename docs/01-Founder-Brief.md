# 1. Vision

MG Supermart is not just a grocery store.

Our vision is to become the most trusted digital-first neighborhood supermarket for Tier-3 and Tier-4 India.

We believe that people should experience the convenience of modern commerce without losing the trust of their local supermarket.

Technology should strengthen local businesses, not replace them.

Every design decision should reinforce trust, simplicity and community.

---

# 2. About MG Supermart

MG Supermart is a retail and wholesale supermarket located in Pipra, East Champaran, Bihar.

The business currently serves customers within approximately 5 km of the store.

Customers know us because of our genuine products, transparent pricing and personal relationships.

This website will become our official online presence.

---



# 3. Why This Website Exists

The website has multiple equally important goals.

## Primary Goals

- Allow customers to purchase groceries online.
- Build trust within the local community.
- Become the official identity of MG Supermart.
- Increase repeat customers.
- Grow the WhatsApp Community.
- Increase Google Business visibility.
- Improve Instagram engagement.



## Secondary Goals

- Showcase modern product design.
- Demonstrate software engineering capability.
- Become a flagship portfolio project.
- Create a foundation for future Android apps.

# 6. Brand Position

We are NOT trying to become Blinkit.

We are NOT trying to become Instamart.

We are NOT trying to become BigBasket.

We are building a trusted neighborhood supermarket that happens to have an exceptional digital experience.

---



# 7. Brand Personality

The brand should feel

- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Honest
- Modern
- Family Friendly
- Premium
- Value for Money
- Warm
- Local
- Human

The tone should never feel corporate.

The tone should never feel like a startup trying too hard.

Customers should feel like they are talking to their trusted neighborhood supermarket.

---



# 8. Product Philosophy

Every decision should follow these principles.

Trust before speed.

Community before convenience.

Clarity before complexity.

Long-term relationships before transactions.

Simple experiences over flashy designs.

Accessibility over trends.

Real business over marketing hype.

---



# 9. Core Business Rules

Delivery Radius

5 KM

Minimum Order

₹500

Important Rule

Cooking Oils and Loose Sugar DO NOT count toward the ₹500 minimum order value.

The website should communicate this politely.

Never surprise customers during checkout.

# 18. Non-Goals

Do not become another Blinkit clone.

Do not prioritize flashy animations over usability.

Do not overwhelm users with offers.

Do not overcomplicate checkout.

Do not sacrifice trust for growth.

---



# 19. Long-Term Vision

MG Supermart should become a digital-first retail brand that proves world-class product experiences can be built for rural India.

The website should become the foundation for future products, including Android apps, delivery systems and intelligent retail solutions.

Every future product should inherit the values defined in this document.
