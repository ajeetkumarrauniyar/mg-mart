# 1. Purpose

This document defines the product strategy for MG Supermart.

It explains why customers should choose MG Supermart over nearby grocery stores and generic ecommerce platforms.

This strategy guides every future product decision, design decision and engineering decision.

Whenever multiple solutions exist, choose the one that aligns with this strategy.

---

# 2. Product Mission

To bring the convenience of modern digital commerce to local communities without sacrificing the trust, relationships and familiarity of a neighborhood supermarket.

---

# 3. Product Vision

MG Supermart aims to become the benchmark for digital-first neighborhood supermarkets in Tier-3 and Tier-4 India.

The platform should feel local, trustworthy, modern and effortless.

Technology should simplify shopping while strengthening relationships between the store and its customers.

---

# 4. Product Positioning

MG Supermart is not competing to become India's fastest grocery delivery platform.

It is building the most trusted and community-focused neighborhood supermarket experience.

We compete on:

- Trust
- Convenience
- Community
- Quality
- Simplicity
- Long-term customer relationships

Not on unrealistic delivery promises.

---

# 5. Value Proposition

Customers choose MG Supermart because:

- They already trust the physical store.
- Products are genuine.
- Pricing is transparent.
- Delivery is local.
- The shopping experience is simple.
- Customer support is human.
- The business is part of the local community.

The website should reinforce these strengths instead of pretending to be a national marketplace.

---

# 6. Core Differentiators

## Hyperlocal

We only serve customers within our service area.

Every customer should feel that MG Supermart belongs to their neighborhood.

---

## Trust First

Trust is more important than discounts.

Clear pricing.

Clear delivery policies.

Clear communication.

No hidden conditions.

---

## Community Driven

The website should strengthen the relationship between the business and the local community.

Support:

- WhatsApp Community
- Instagram
- Google Business Profile
- Local campaigns
- Festival greetings

---

## Festival Commerce

Festivals are a major shopping driver in Bihar.

The platform should evolve with the local calendar.

Instead of temporary banners, festivals should become complete shopping experiences.

Examples include:

- Shravan
- Raksha Bandhan
- Janmashtami
- Navratri
- Durga Puja
- Dussehra
- Diwali
- Chhath Puja
- Wedding Season

---

## Seasonal Experience Engine

The product architecture should support future implementation of the Seasonal Experience Engine (SEE).

This capability will allow the platform to automatically adapt its content, merchandising and campaigns based on seasons, festivals and local events.

- Snacks

# 11. Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of these principles:

- Increase trust.
- Reduce customer effort.
- Strengthen local identity.
- Improve shopping convenience.
- Encourage repeat purchases.
- Support future scalability.

If a feature does not support any of these principles, it should not be built.

---

# 12. What We Will Never Do

We will never copy another grocery platform.

We will never sacrifice usability for visual effects.

We will never overload customers with banners and advertisements.

We will never use dark UX patterns.

We will never prioritize growth over customer trust.

We will always design for long-term relationships.
