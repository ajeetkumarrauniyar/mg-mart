# Business Operations Principles

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** 16 July 2026

---

# Purpose

Ye document MG SuperMart ko chalane ke basic rules define karta hai.

Ye rules har din follow honge.

---

# Our Goal

- Customer khush rahe.
- Store hamesha ready rahe.
- Stock kabhi bina planning ke khatam na ho.
- Kaam aadmi ke bharose nahi, system ke bharose chale.

---

# Customer First

□ Customer ko welcome karo.

□ Customer ko ignore mat karo.

□ Agar product nahi hai to alternative suggest karo.

□ Har customer ko bill do.

□ Return aur complaint politely handle karo.

□ Counter par unnecessary mobile use mat karo.

---

# Store Rules

□ Store khulne se pehle safai complete.

□ Shelf khali nahi dikhni chahiye.

□ Price tag visible hona chahiye.

□ Expired product shelf par nahi hona chahiye.

□ Damaged packing display par nahi rakhni hai.

□ Counter hamesha clean rahe.

---

# Stock Rules

□ Fast moving products kabhi Out of Stock nahi hone chahiye.

□ FIFO follow karo.

□ Near expiry products pehle nikalo.

□ Dead stock har month identify karo.

□ Festival stock pehle se prepare karo.

□ Scheme dekhkar nahi, demand dekhkar purchase karo.

---

# Purchase Rules

□ Purchase planning weekly hogi.

□ Emergency purchase avoid karo.

□ Distributor scheme check karo.

□ Purchase record same day update karo.

□ Receiving ke time quantity verify karo.

□ Damaged goods alag rakho.

---

# Billing Rules

□ Billing fast aur accurate honi chahiye.

□ Cash receive hone ke baad hi product handover karo.

□ QR payment verify karo.

□ Duplicate billing avoid karo.

□ Wholesale aur Retail billing alag maintain karo.

---

# Cash Rules

□ Counter cash daily tally karo.

□ Extra cash counter par mat rakho.

□ Cash aur UPI mismatch same din solve karo.

□ Cash closing bina delay complete karo.

---

# Cleaning Rules

□ Daily sweeping.

□ Counter cleaning.

□ Glass cleaning.

□ Weekly rack cleaning.

□ Monthly deep cleaning.

□ Pest control time par.

---

# Display Rules

□ Entrance attractive rakho.

□ Festival products front par.

□ Offer products eye level par.

□ High margin products visible rakho.

□ Empty hooks refill karo.

---

# Festival Rules

□ Festival se pehle planning.

□ Festival ke hisaab se display.

□ Festival ke hisaab se stock.

□ Festival ke baad review.

---

# Team Rules

□ Har kaam ka owner clear hona chahiye.

□ Ek hi kaam do log bina reason ke na karein.

□ Important baatein likh kar rakhein.

□ Jo kaam complete ho usko tick karo.

---

# Accounting Rules

□ Sales entry daily.

□ Purchase entry daily.

□ GST work pending mat rakho.

□ Outstanding review weekly.

□ Bank reconciliation weekly.

---

# Safety Rules

□ Fire extinguisher accessible.

□ First aid box available.

□ Electric switch board safe.

□ Emergency numbers display par.

---

# Review Rules

Har Sunday review karo:

□ Sales

□ Purchase

□ Profit

□ Fast Movers

□ Slow Movers

□ Dead Stock

□ Near Expiry

□ Pending Payments

□ Store Cleanliness

---

# One Line Rule

> **"Aaj ka kaam aaj hi khatam karo. Kal ke bharose dukaan nahi chalti."**
