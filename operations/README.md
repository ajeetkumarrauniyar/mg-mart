# MG SuperMart Operations Manual

## Purpose

This manual is the operating system for running MG SuperMart.

It is designed to help manage day-to-day operations, seasonal planning, business reviews and recurring business activities without relying on memory.

The goal is not to document everything.

The goal is to make the right business decisions at the right time.

---

## How to Use

### Daily

Open:

- Command Center
- Daily Operations

---

### Weekly

Open:

- Weekly Operations
- Master Calendar

---

### Monthly

Open:

- Accounts
- Business Review

---

### Before Every Festival

1. Check the Master Calendar.
2. Open the relevant Festival Playbook.
3. Follow the checklist.
4. Complete the Festival Review after the season ends.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Keep documentation simple.
- Only document things that are repeatedly useful.
- Review and improve the manual using real business experience.
- Remove anything that is no longer useful.
- This manual should evolve with the business.

---

## Scope

This manual currently covers:

- Daily Operations
- Weekly Operations
- Festival Planning
- Marketing
- Accounts
- Business Review

Procurement has intentionally been excluded because it is managed separately.
