# Festival Playbooks

**Purpose**

Har festival ki tayari time par aur bina bhag-daud ke complete karna.

Ye folder sirf festival-specific kaam ke liye hai.

Roz is folder ko dekhne ki zarurat nahi hai.

Festival aane par hi relevant file kholo.

---

# Kaise Use Kare

## Step 1

Master Calendar me dekho agla festival kaunsa hai.

↓

## Step 2

Us festival ki file kholo.

↓

## Step 3

Timeline ke hisaab se checklist complete karo.

↓

## Step 4

Festival ke baad review complete karo.
