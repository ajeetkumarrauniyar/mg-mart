01

Brand DNA

ब्रांड की आत्मा

Everything in this document grows from one word. Not a slogan — a feeling every customer already has when they walk into the shop in Pipra, which every screen must now reproduce.

अपनापन
apnāpan — the warmth of being known

The shopkeeper who remembers your family, rounds off the odd rupee, and tells you honestly when the atta is not fresh. Big platforms sell speed and discounts; nobody in Tier-3 India trusts them the way they trust their own shop. Apnapan is the one asset Blinkit, Zepto and Amazon cannot copy — so the entire design language is engineered to carry it onto a screen.

The banyan model · बरगद क्यों?

Every village and qasba has one: the bargad under which the market sits. It is the local architecture of trust — and the structural model for this system.

जड़ें Roots

Tokens — color, type, spacing, radius. Fixed and deep. They never change with campaigns, and no screen may invent values outside them.

तना Trunk

Components — buttons, cards, forms, navigation. One recognizable body shared by the website, admin panel and future apps.

छाँव Canopy

Experiences — pages, festivals, campaigns. The canopy flowers with every season (Chhath, Diwali, Holi) while roots and trunk stay still.

Personality, measured · ताकि “warm” बहस न बने

Warm गर्मजोशीCool / clinical
Familiar जाना-पहचानाNovel / experimental
Calm शांतEnergetic / loud
Honest value सही दामLuxury signalling
Local अपने मोहल्ले काGlobal / placeless

“Premium” here means craft — generous space, careful type, honest photography — never luxury cues like gold foil, thin serifs on black, or minimal-to-the-point-of-cold layouts.

Voice — the shopkeeper test · दुकानदार की ज़बान

✓ ऐसा बोलें — say this
“नमस्ते! त्योहार की तैयारी शुरू करें।”
“हम कल शाम 5–7 बजे के बीच पहुँचा देंगे।”
“तेल और खुली चीनी ₹500 की न्यूनतम राशि में नहीं गिने जाते — पहले ही बता देना ठीक लगा।”
“Your order is packed. Ramesh is on the way.”
✗ ऐसा कभी नहीं — never this
“MEGA BLOWOUT SALE!!! 80% OFF!!!”
“Hurry! Only 2 left!” (when it is not true)
“10-minute delivery” — promises we borrow from apps we are not
Machine-translated Hindi: “आपका गाड़ी खाली है”
क्यों · WHY
Trust psychology is boring on purpose: people trust what is consistent, unhurried and transparent. Loud interfaces read as desperate; calm ones read as established. Every DNA trait therefore maps to a concrete UI behavior — Warm → cream surfaces and a serif voice; Honest → policies stated before checkout, never during; Calm → one primary action per screen; Local → Hindi as a first-class script, never a fallback; Human → real photographs of the real shop. If a screen cannot pass the shopkeeper test (“would Ajeet say this across the counter?”), it does not ship.

The seal works like a मुहर (stamp): stamps mean authenticity and receipts, not marketing. And the strict two-colorway rule exists because identity recognition is a memory game — every extra variant taxes the memory of a first-time internet user.
03

Color philosophy

रंगों का मतलब

Color is vocabulary, not decoration. Many of our customers are first-time internet users; some read slowly. For them a consistent color is a word — so every color in Bargad has exactly one meaning, used the same way on every screen, forever. This ledger is locked by Design Principles §6.

Banyan green — freshness, success, “this is the right way”. Primary actions, links, confirmations.
Haldi yellow — offers and highlights. Good news about money, never alarm.
Sindoor red — errors and true urgency only. Never prices, never discounts, never marketing.
Asmani blue — information: policies, delivery notes, helpful explanations.
Mitti terracotta — community and festival warmth. Emotional, never functional.
Kajal & Khadi neutrals — content and structure. The quiet 90% of every screen.

Proportion — the 70 / 20 / 10 rule

≈70% warm air
Malai & white
≈20% structure
ink, borders, photos
≈10%
brand

If a screen feels “off-brand”, it is almost always this ratio broken — too much green, too many accents. The brand lives in the 10%, which is why it stays powerful.

Deep, not bright

High saturation is the color of hurry — flash sales, 10-minute apps. Depth is the color of establishment. Our green is a banyan leaf in shade, not a neon “ORDER NOW”.

Warm, not white

Pure #FFFFFF glares on cheap panels and in outdoor sun, where our customers actually shop. Malai reads like paper, calms the page, and makes food photography look appetizing.

One accent per view

A bazaar shouts from every stall; a trusted shop speaks once. A screen may use one accent family besides green. Two is a warning; three is a bug.

क्यों · WHY
Every hue is borrowed from materials our customers already trust — banyan leaf, malai, haldi, mitti, kajal, sindoor, the sky. Borrowed color carries borrowed meaning: haldi is already auspicious, mitti already feels like home. It also gives the team a shared language (“make it haldi, not sindoor”) that survives translation between designers, developers and the family running the shop. Trend palettes (neon gradients, purple-blue SaaS) would place us in the startup shelf and age in three years; earth doesn't go out of fashion.

सहायक रंग

क्यों · WHY
Haldi because turmeric and marigold are already शुभ — an offer wrapped in haldi feels like good fortune, not pressure; the same offer in red feels like a trap. The payoff is compounding: when every red is real, one red banner earns instant attention — and when nothing screams, customers stop bracing for tricks. That felt safety is the whole brand.

Typography system

अक्षर-व्यवस्था

Two voices. Martel (serif) is the brand speaking — hero lines, festival greetings, storytelling. Mukta (sans) is the shop working — prices, buttons, forms, everything functional. Both families were designed Devanagari-first, so Hindi is never a fallback font wearing borrowed clothes.

Composition rules
Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps, ever — Devanagari has no capitals, so caps would make English shout while Hindi whispers.
No italics — Devanagari has none. Emphasis = weight or Banyan color.
Never letter-space Devanagari; matras break apart.
Measure: 45–70 characters English, ≈55 Hindi.
Numbers & floors
Prices in tabular numerals, Latin digits (₹68 — matching shop receipts), ₹ always attached to the amount.
Hindi body gets +2 line-height steps (16/28 vs 16/26) — matras need headroom.
13 px is the absolute floor. Body is 16 px so browsers never auto-zoom and elderly customers never squint.
क्यों · WHY
A serif for the brand voice carries the authority of print — newspapers, schoolbooks, painted boards — the materials rural India already trusts. A tall-x-height humanist sans for the working voice maximizes legibility at 14–16 px on low-resolution screens. Two families is the ceiling: every additional font costs kilobytes on slow connections and coherence in the mind. The system reads as one calm speaker with two registers — the same shopkeeper, sometimes warm, sometimes efficient.

09

Grid system

ढाँचा

Everything sits on a 4 px baseline. Columns scale from 4 on mobile — the canonical layout — to 12 on desktop, with content capped at 1200 px. The same grid governs the website, admin panel and future app, so teams and customers only ever learn one geometry.

0 – 599 · Mobile4 columns · 16 px margins · 12 px gutters — the canonical layout
600 – 904 · Tablet8 columns · 24 px margins · 16 px gutters
905 – 1239 · Desktop12 columns · 32 px margins · 24 px gutters
1240+ · Wide12 columns · content capped at 1200 px, centered · 24 px gutters
Product grid2-up mobile → 3-up tablet → 4/5-up desktop, always with 12–16 px gutters
क्यों · WHY
A 4 px base (not 8) because our touch minimums — 48, 52, 56 — and dense grocery grids need the finer step without breaking rhythm. The 1200 px cap keeps line lengths readable and the page at the scale of a shop, not a warehouse: edge-to-edge desktop layouts read as “platform”, and we are deliberately not one. Mobile is designed first and never adapted down — 90%+ of our customers will only ever see the 4-column layout, so it is the original, not the compromise.

Spacing scale

साँस लेने की जगह

Nine steps on the 4 px base, shown at actual size. Spacing is how the interface breathes — and generous breathing room is the cheapest premium signal there is.

{{ s.k }} {{ s.v }} px {{ s.use }}
Proximity = relationship

8 px inside an element · 16 px between related things · 24–32 px between groups · 48–64 px between page sections. Spacing does the explaining so borders and boxes don't have to.

Touch floors

48 px minimum tap target, 56 px for money actions, ≥8 px between adjacent targets. Forms never compress below 16 px vertical rhythm — dense grocery grids may drop to 12 px gutters, nothing else may.

क्यों · WHY
Crowding reads as bazaar; air reads as confidence. For elderly customers and first-time smartphone users, whitespace is also motor accessibility — fat fingers on small screens need forgiveness between targets. The named scale ends design-by-eyeball: when every gap is a token, screens built by different people in different years still feel like one shop.

Border radius

कोमल कोने

Five steps. Soft enough to feel human, restrained enough to stay serious.

{{ r.k }}
{{ r.use }}
Nesting rule

Corners step down one level as you nest: card r-16 → image tile r-12 → tag r-8. Never equal or larger inside — mismatched nesting is the fastest way to look hand-made in the bad sense.

Pill discipline

Full pills are for small, grabbable things — chips, steppers, badges. Buttons and inputs stay r-12: pill-shaped primary buttons are the signature of the very apps we refuse to resemble.

क्यों · WHY
Sharp corners read as institutional and cold; over-round corners read as toy-like and trendy — both fail “timeless”. 12–16 px is the warmth of a worn shop counter: soft where hands touch, straight where structure matters. One geometry runs through everything — rounded rectangle surfaces, round-capped icons, open round letterforms in Mukta — so the whole interface feels carved from the same wood.

Elevation & shadow

छाँव के स्तर

Four levels, named after the tree. Most of the interface rests on the ground and is separated by borders; shadow is earned only by things that truly float above the page while you scroll.

{{ e.k }}
{{ e.use }}
Shadow recipe

Always warm ink — rgba(36,32,26, …) — never blue-black, never colored glows. Soft, downward, honest about a single light source, like afternoon light through leaves.

Border-first separation

Cheap panels band and posterize large soft shadows. A crisp 1 px khadi-200 border costs nothing, renders perfectly on every screen, and keeps the page calm. In dark mode, elevation becomes surface lightness — shadows disappear.

क्यों · WHY
Deep drop-shadows are drama, and drama is the opposite of calm. Reserving Level 3 for one sheet per screen preserves a physical logic even first-time users grasp instantly: what floats is what you're holding right now. Everything else stays on the counter.

Rules
Icons never travel alone — navigation and actions always pair icon + word.

And choosing a maintained open-source set over bespoke drawing means the future Android app and admin panel inherit hundreds of consistent glyphs for free.

Motion

शांत हरकत

Motion is a shopkeeper's hands: quick, sure, never theatrical. Five principles — purposeful (guides the eye), quick (≤320 ms), grounded (things slide and settle; nothing bounces), honest (skeletons over spinners, progress never fake), respectful (reduced-motion collapses everything to fades).

easing enter/standard cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) · exit cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1) · springs and overshoot: never

डिलीवरी का समय चुनें · Choose delivery slot
आज शाम 5–7 कल सुबह 8–10 कल शाम 5–7

Sheet rises 320 ms decelerating, scrim fades in step. Exit is faster than entry (240 ms) — leaving should feel lighter than arriving.

Performance is the budget

Animate only transform and opacity. One thing moves at a time. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no looping decoration. On a ₹6,000 phone, a dropped frame costs more trust than a beautiful transition earns.

One celebration

The system celebrates exactly once: when an order is placed, a 600 ms glow of the success tick — like a diya catching flame. Played once, never looped. Rarity is what makes it felt.

क्यों · WHY
Motion is the body language of the interface. Bouncy overshoot says “startup demo”; instant snapping says “broken”; a short settled slide says “handled with care” — the register of a shop that packs your bag properly. Skeletons over spinners because a spinner admits nothing is happening, while a skeleton shows the shape of what's coming; on slow networks that difference is the difference between waiting and leaving. And celebration is rationed for the same reason red is: scarcity is what keeps meaning.
Part C — Components · घटक
17

Component philosophy

हर घटक एक दुकानदार

Every component must behave the way a good shopkeeper behaves. Seven behaviors, testable on every piece of UI before it ships:

1 · Greets clearly

Labels are always visible — on fields, on icons, on states. Nothing relies on memory or guessing.

2 · Speaks both languages

Hindi and English are content slots in every component, budgeted from day one — never a retrofit.

3 · Honest about money

₹ totals always visible, fees explained before checkout, the ₹500 rule stated in the cart — never at payment.

4 · Fits every hand

48 px targets, 56 px for money. Designed for thumbs, trembling hands, and phones used in sunlight.

5 · Patient

No timeouts, no countdowns, forgiving inputs (spaces in phone numbers, Hindi or English search). Nobody is rushed.

6 · Works on slow days

Every component specifies its loading, empty, offline and error states. 2G is a design constraint, not an edge case.

7 · Never pressures

No dark patterns, no fake scarcity, no pre-ticked boxes, no guilt copy. Ever. (Product Strategy §12.)

Definition of done

A component ships only with: anatomy · all 9 states (default, hover, press, focus, disabled, loading, error, empty, offline) · both languages tested · contrast audited · keyboard & screen-reader paths · token-only styling.

क्यों · WHY
Components are where philosophy becomes muscle memory. A principles document nobody can test is poetry; a checklist every button must pass is a system. Framing the checklist as a shopkeeper keeps it honest for everyone — the founder can audit a screen without knowing what a “focus state” is, by asking whether the screen behaves like his own counter.

Responsive strategy

हर स्क्रीन पर

One system that re-arranges, never two designs. Mobile is the original; desktop is the same shop with a wider counter. Nothing exists on desktop that a mobile customer cannot do.

NavigationBottom bar + FABs → top bar + category row with mega-menu
Product grid2-up → 3-up → 4/5-up; card anatomy identical at every size
OverlaysBottom sheets → centered dialogs and popovers

Money actionsSticky full-width bar → inline button beside the order summary
HoverEnhancement only — nothing is revealed exclusively on hover

क्यों · WHY
The overwhelming majority of customers are Android-mobile (Founder Brief §5) — so mobile is designed first and desktop derives from it, never the reverse. Feature parity is a trust rule as much as a technical one: a family shares one account across a phone and a school laptop, and the shop must be the same shop on both. One re-arranging system is also the only thing a small team can actually maintain for years — two diverging designs is how brands rot.
